Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

Are all notes and papers free?

Yes! Everything on SabkaCode — notes, previous papers, and exam solutions — is 100% free with no registration required.

Which universities are covered?

Currently AKTU, CSJMU, DBRAU and CCSU are covered. More universities are being added regularly.

How do I request content removal?

Send an email to sabkacode@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright / Content Removal". We will take action within 24–48 hours.

Can I suggest new content?

Absolutely! Fill in the form above or send us a message on WhatsApp. We welcome all suggestions from students.

How can I collaborate with SabkaCode?

For collaboration or partnership enquiries, send an email to sabkacode@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How long does it take to get a reply?

We aim to reply on social media within 2–6 hours and respond to emails within 24–48 hours on working days.
